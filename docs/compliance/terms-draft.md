# Pilot terms — draft

**Status:** draft for the WP2 legal/privacy review. **Date:** 2026-11-19.
Terms for taking part in the phase A pilot (`pilot-plan.md`). Bracketed text is a
placeholder. Not legal advice.

**How to use it:** these terms are shown once, before the first session, with a
checkbox. They describe a free prototype test between the pilot owner and a
volunteer; they are deliberately short, because the pilot is small and
synthetic. They must not promise anything the code does not do.

### 2. Who can take part

You must be 18 or over and taking part voluntarily. You must not be under any
obligation to test the product, and you can stop at any time, for any reason,
without giving one.

### 3. It is a prototype

The software is unfinished and changes without notice. It may be unavailable, it
may lose data, and it may show wrong information. We do not promise that it
works, that it is available, or that it will still exist after the pilot.

Do not rely on it for a real deadline. If you have a real application to file,
use the official portal, not RINAVI.

### 4. Use practice documents only

During the pilot you must upload only the **SPECIMEN — SYNTHETIC** documents we
give you. You must not upload, type or dictate:

- a real Aadhaar number or any other real identity number;
- a real proof of address, income or identity belonging to you or anyone else;
- anyone else's personal information;
- a portal password, OTP code or CAPTCHA answer.

If you do, we will stop the session and delete the upload. This is a condition
of taking part, not a suggestion: the prototype is not built to hold real
documents safely.

Keep your passkey and your device secure. You are responsible for activity under
your account. Do not share your sign-in with another participant. You can delete
your account at any time from the app; deletion is immediate and permanent.

- Try to access another participant's data, or anyone else's account.
- Probe, scan or attack the system, or attempt to get around its limits.
- Copy, resell or redistribute the prototype or the practice documents.
- Use the prototype for anything unlawful.

We may end your participation if you do any of these.

affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any government department. We do not
