## RINAVI privacy notice (pilot)

### 1. Who we are

RINAVI is a prototype built by **[entity name]**, [city], India. For this pilot
we are the people who decide why your information is used, which makes us the
"data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

You can reach us at **[contact email]**. Complaints go to **[grievance officer
name and email]**.

### 2. What RINAVI is not

RINAVI is **not a government website**. We are not the e-District portal, we are
not the Delhi Government, and we cannot approve, reject, speed up or influence
any application.

RINAVI prepares a draft. **You file it yourself on the official portal.** We
never submit anything for you, and we never ask for your portal password, an OTP
or a CAPTCHA answer.

### 3. What we ask you for

- **An email address**, so you can sign in.
- **A passkey**, if you choose to set one up. Only the public half is stored;
  the private half stays on your device.
- **Two practice documents** that we give you. They are marked
  **SPECIMEN — SYNTHETIC**. They are not real documents and they are not yours.
- **What you do in the app**: the drafts you create and the case details you
  record.

### 4. Why we use it — four permissions

We ask for each of these separately, and you can say no to any of them. If you
say no, the matching part of the app stops working, and nothing else does.

| Permission | What it allows | What stops without it |
|---|---|---|
| **Document processing** | Reading a document you upload so its fields can be pulled out | You cannot upload or read documents |
| **Profile storage** | Keeping those values in your profile so you can check them | You cannot save or review profile values |
| **Application preparation** | Filling a draft from your profile and documents | You cannot create or export a draft |
| **Application tracking** | Recording what happened to an application you filed yourself | You cannot record a case |

You can withdraw any permission on the **Consent** page. Withdrawing stops
the next use; it does not delete what is already stored. Delete the item, or
your account, for that.

### 5. What we never ask for, and what we never promote

- Your real Aadhaar number as a profile field. If a document you upload happens
  to contain one, we recognise the line so the document can be sorted, but we
  do not keep the number: it is replaced with a marker before anything is
  saved. We do not add it to your profile, show it in a draft, put it in your
  packet or include it in an export. The number is still inside the file you
  uploaded, so delete the document to erase it. The pilot uses SPECIMEN numbers
  only.
- Portal passwords, OTP codes or CAPTCHA answers.
- Your location, contact list, photos or files other than the two you upload.
- Advertising or tracking identifiers. There is no analytics or advertising
  software in RINAVI, and no third-party tracker.

### 6. Who sees your information

Only you, and the person running the pilot. There is no admin panel and no
support team with a login. Other participants cannot see your data; every screen
is limited to your own account.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else. We use
no outside company to store, analyse or process it, and no AI service reads your
documents — the extraction is ordinary pattern matching on our own machine.

### 7. Where it is kept

On a single computer used for the pilot, in **[city]**, India. The document
files you upload are stored encrypted, but the database that holds your account
and the details RINAVI reads out of those files is not, and the key to the files
sits on the same computer — so encryption guards against a stray copy of a file,
not against someone who takes the whole machine. It is not backed up; if the
machine fails, the data is gone. Those limits are one reason the pilot uses fake
documents only.

### 8. How long we keep it

- **Your account and everything in it:** until you delete it. Deletion is
  immediate and permanent.
- **A document:** until you delete it. The file is erased first, then the record.
- **Security records** (for example "a document was uploaded"): kept after you
  delete your account, with the link to you removed, so we can still show how
  the system was used.
- **Session:** you stay signed in for up to 7 days, or until you sign out.

The full detail, including what is not yet automated, is in our retention policy
at **[link]**.

### 9. Your choices

- **See what we hold:** the app shows your documents, profile, drafts and cases.
- **Correct it:** you can correct a profile value, and the correction is
  recorded.
- **Export it:** you can download your account record and your permission
  history. **This export does not yet include your documents or profile
  values** — you can read them in the app, but not download them as a file. We
  would rather say so than let you assume otherwise.
- **Delete it:** delete a document, or delete your account. Account deletion
  erases your documents, profile, drafts and cases.
- **Withdraw a permission:** on the Consent page.
- **Complain:** write to **[grievance officer]**. We will reply within
  **[30] days**. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data
  Protection Board of India.

### 10. About this pilot

This is a test of a prototype, not a service. You are one of 10–20 volunteers.
Sessions are 25–30 minutes and we may take notes, and record audio or video if
you separately agree — that agreement is a different form
(`recording-consent.md`), and saying no to it does not stop you taking part.

If you accidentally bring a real document, we will stop, not upload it, and
delete anything already uploaded if you ask us to.

### 11. Changes

If we change this notice, we will show the new version in the app before using
your information for anything new. This version is **1.0**, dated
**[date]**.

### 12. Questions

**[contact email]** — we will answer in English. The app itself is English-only
in this pilot.
